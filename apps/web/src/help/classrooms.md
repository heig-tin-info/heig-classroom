# My classrooms

## What is a classroom

A classroom binds a **GitHub organization** to a **student roster**. Assignments created in it are distributed as private repositories inside that organization, one per student.

## How to switch views

Use the view switch above the list: **cards**, a **sortable list** or the **timeline**. The search field filters as you type (fuzzy match on name and organization).

## How to open things

- Click a card to open the classroom.
- Click the organization name to open it on GitHub.
- Hover the student count to see who is enrolled and who has already claimed their seat.
- The sidebar lists your classrooms: one click to any of them from any page.

## How to see archived classrooms

Click the **archive** button next to the view switch. Archived classrooms appear as muted cards with their archive date; **Restore** brings one back among the active classrooms. Archiving never deletes anything — data and GitHub repositories are kept.

## How to test the student experience

Open a classroom, go to the **Students** tab and use **Join as student**: you take a *staff* seat (excluded from the headcount). Then pick **Switch to student view** in the account menu (bottom of the sidebar) to see the real student view — accept the assignment, push, watch your grade.
