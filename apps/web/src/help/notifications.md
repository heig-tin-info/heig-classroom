# Notifications

## In-app toasts

Real-time toasts appear at the bottom right when something that concerns you happens. Toggle each kind here — this setting is stored **in this browser**.

- **Students** hear only about their own repositories and those of their group: acceptance, push, grade, restored protected files.
- **Staff** hear about the classrooms they currently teach, never about archived ones: deadlines enforced, syncs and LLM reviews on every such classroom, plus the student activity (a student joins, accepts, pushes, is graded) of the classrooms where **Notify me about students** is on.

## Per classroom

The **Notify me about students** switch sits on the Settings tab of each classroom and is stored **on your account**. It is on by default for the owner of the classroom and off for co-teachers and assistants. Turned off, the pages still update live; only the toasts stop.

## E-mail notifications

The next section configures e-mails (new assignment, deadline reminder, final grade, provisioning failures…). Those preferences are stored **on your account** and follow you across devices; every message also carries a one-click unsubscribe link for its kind.
